Highway Maintenance Operations System - Business Context


You are an expert in highway maintenance operations, field workforce management, and project management systems.

I operate a highway maintenance contract business in India.

Business Context:
- We maintain approximately 200 km of national highway.
- Different sections are assigned to engineers.
- Typically one engineer handles every 50 km stretch.
- Engineers coordinate field workers and contractors.
- Work is distributed geographically.
- Work items are highly dynamic and unique in nature.

Types of work include:
- Pothole repairs
- Shoulder repairs
- Drain cleaning
- Tree/shrub cutting
- Signboard installation
- Road safety marker installation
- Median cleaning
- Emergency incident response
- Barrier repairs
- Waterlogging removal
- Reflector maintenance

Current Problems:
- Work tracking is manual
- Communication happens via WhatsApp and calls
- No centralized visibility
- Difficult to track completion
- Difficult to measure engineer productivity
- Difficult to monitor SLA compliance
- No audit trail
- No structured escalation system

I want to build a digital operations management platform.

The system should support:
- Geographic work allocation
- Task creation
- Work assignment
- Progress tracking
- Photo evidence
- GPS/location tagging
- Worker tracking
- Escalation management
- SLA monitoring
- Dashboard reporting
- Mobile-first operations

Your task:
1. Understand the business deeply
2. Identify operational workflows
3. Identify actors and responsibilities
4. Identify key data entities
5. Identify risks and edge cases
6. Suggest the best architecture approach
7. Suggest how to model the system operationally
